About

Two disciplines, one build style.

I come from an accounting background — audit traineeship, hotel accounts, and finance work at Oak Ray Group of Companies — before moving into application and web development. That combination shows up in the products I build: payroll logic that respects ETF/EPF rules, billing systems with real multi-currency and BOM tracking, and dashboards accountants would actually trust.

Most of my work is single-file HTML/JS apps and Java desktop tools, deployed straight to Firebase, Netlify, or GitHub Pages. I favor direct, working files over long write-ups — you ask, I build, you get the file.
