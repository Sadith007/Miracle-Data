About

Two disciplines, one build style.

I come from an accounting background before moving into application and web development — and it still shapes how I build.

I started in finance: an audit traineeship, hotel accounts work, and time at Oak Ray Group of Companies. Reconciliations, VAT/EPF/ETF compliance, QuickBooks — the unglamorous groundwork of making numbers actually correct.

That's the lens I bring to development now. When I build payroll logic, I know ETF is employer-side only under Sri Lankan law. When I build a billing system, I know what a real invoice workflow needs — multi-currency, BOM tracking, print formats an accountant would recognize.

Most of my work today is single-file HTML/JS apps and Java desktop tools, deployed straight to Firebase, Netlify, or GitHub Pages, under the Miracle Data name. I iterate fast and prefer handing over a working file over a long explanation.
